THE STORY BEHIND HAUS OF NITER

Built on Legacy. Designed with Purpose.

Brittney Niter

Founder | Web Developer | Creative Entrepreneur

Welcome to Haus of Niter, where dreams come true. I'm Brittney Niter, the founder of Haus of Niter, a creative business built on family legacy, determination, and a passion for helping others bring their visions to life.

The name Niter comes from my father, who owned his own roofing and construction business in Memphis, Tennessee. He built his business using the knowledge and skills he had, making sure families had strong, secure roofs over their heads.

While my father built roofs to protect homes, I'm building digital foundations to help businesses grow.

With a background in Computer Science and a passion for creativity, I help entrepreneurs and small businesses turn their ideas into professional websites, custom designs, and memorable brands.

Haus of Niter is more than just a name. To me, it represents quality, creativity, and the belief that every dream deserves an opportunity to become something real.

My father's journey taught me that you don't have to start with everything. You start with what you know, work with dedication, and build something meaningful.

Website Revision Policy

We want you to love your finished website! Each website package includes a set number of revision rounds.

  • Basic Website: 1 revision round included
  • Small Business Website: 2 revision rounds included
  • Expanded Business Website: 2 revision rounds included

Each revision round includes one combined list of minor changes, such as updating text, colors, images, or layout details within the agreed design.

Additional minor revision rounds start at $25. More extensive changes start at $50 and may require a separate quote. New pages, major redesigns, and additional features are priced separately.

Corrections to errors made by Haus of Niter or fixes needed to meet the agreed project requirements are provided at no extra charge.
